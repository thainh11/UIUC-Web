# Page design

The page is one scroll, 70rem wide. A sticky header reads "UIUC Research" and links to Overview, Output, Topics, Papers, and Faculty. On a narrow screen those links sit in one horizontal row.

The order is the order of a campus profile: who the university is, a few large numbers, where the work sits, then the papers and the people. The hero is the photograph "Main Quad - UIUC" by Daderot ([Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Main_Quad_-_UIUC_-_DSC09116.JPG), public domain), the title "Research at Illinois", the line "Research output, last two years", and a pin for Urbana-Champaign, Illinois, which opens OpenStreetMap. Every total names its date window, so the figures stay inside these two years. The five cards are papers, UIUC author profiles, citations to these papers, the open-access share, and faculty. "UIUC author profiles" counts OpenAlex author ids on UIUC authorships, and one person can have more than one id. The faculty card adds a second line for how many of the directory have at least one linked paper.

The monthly chart uses a line, loaded as its own chunk when that section is reached. The first and last months are partial, so those ends are dashed, with open dots, and the caption says so. Papers dated 1 January (about 650 each year; any other day has a median of 24 and at most 157) nearly always carry only a publication year, so the line leaves them out and a note gives their number; the data table lists them per month. Recharts' own keyboard layer is off: the chart is a picture with a text summary plus the table. Focus areas are buttons, twelve at first and the rest behind "Show all". The bar width is that field's count over the largest count. Choosing a field filters the paper list and moves focus there. Choosing it again clears the filter. The selected bar uses orange plus a blue border, and `aria-pressed`.

A paper row shows the title, date, the first six authors, topic, citation count, and an open-access mark. A DOI opens in a new tab. Linked faculty are chips that open the drawer. The names in the author list are plain text: that payload has no author id. Sort is by date, by citations, or by relevance once a query is typed. Page size runs from 1 to 50, default 20.

Faculty cards show the photo, name, position, linked-paper count, and up to three fields taken from those papers. Directory keywords stay off the card. They are long, uneven, and often beside the point (one person can list hundreds, including phrases that are not their field), so a card ranked by keyword text would be hard to scan. The drawer shows position, email, fields from the linked papers, eight keywords with the rest behind "Show all", and up to 100 linked papers, newest first. A missing photo becomes initials. "Ask the assistant about this person" closes the drawer and opens chat with that faculty id as `context`.

On a screen at least 64rem wide, chat is a 380px panel in the corner and the page behind it still works. Below that width the panel covers the screen. An empty thread offers the two questions above. With a profile attached, the samples become questions about that person. Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line, and Stop aborts the stream. A paper source opens its DOI. A faculty source closes chat and opens the drawer. One failed turn leaves the rest of the thread in place, and Try again sends the same question.

Text and primary buttons are Illini Blue `#13294B` on white. Illini Orange `#FF5F05` is for the selected bar, the hero rule, and similar marks. Orange on white is about 3.05:1, so small text stays blue (`#13294B`) or muted (`#475569` on white, about 7.6:1). Titles use Newsreader. Labels, tables, and the chat log use Atkinson Hyperlegible, self-hosted. Icons are Phosphor, 20px, outline. Motion is a short fade, and `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the movement. The footer names OpenAlex (CC0) for the papers, the assignment directory for faculty, and the campus photo.
